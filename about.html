About Glame Chic

Glame Chic is a luxury feminine beauty brand based in Oran, Algeria. The brand offers curated makeup collections, beauty boxes, and authentic cosmetics selected for modern women who want elegance, quality, and confidence.

Our mission is to provide premium beauty products that combine style, authenticity, and accessibility for women across Algeria.

Glame Chic gained strong visibility through social media platforms where thousands of customers discover our products daily.
